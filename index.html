Using the keypad

  • Enter your code on the keypad on the door.
  • Wait for the blue check on the bottom-left button with the house icon. That's it unlocking.
  • To lock, push the bottom-right button with the lock icon. It also locks itself after four minutes.
  • Arriving in the dark? Press the bottom-left of the keypad to light it up.
